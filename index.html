Makaseb · Alraqia / Bouncy Toy

Turning a store problem into a customer-journey problem.

A large Saudi retailer selling stationery, office supplies and children's toys. We inherited a broad catalog that needed a clearer information architecture — and I later used a winning product to investigate why good funnel activity was not becoming enough sales.

Store restructuring

Start with the hierarchy.

Worked with the Account Director to define a roadmap, then used Salla tools to reorganize categories, product placement and the overall store flow.

The Bouncy Toy case

The funnel looked healthy — sales did not.

The product was getting attention and moving through the funnel, but sales were weak. Microsoft Clarity showed sessions spending unusually long on the product page before leaving.

Hypothesis

Maybe customers were missing information.

I proposed a short message campaign to hear the real objections instead of guessing from ad metrics alone.

What we learned

The questions were about the product itself.

Customers asked whether the electric inflator was included, how installation worked and whether the actual size would match expectations.

Decision path
Data → user behaviour → hypothesis → customer questions → product-page improvement.

Makaseb · MOKNH

From price-led offers to value-led growth.

A new wellness brand with one core product. The initial idea was to win with a lower price than similar products. I challenged the assumption and used the early result to shift the offer toward trust, perceived value and creator-led demand.

+25%sales uplift after offer repositioning
1core product
UGCcreator-led credibility
Valueinstead of discount-only messaging
Initial assumption

Lower price = easier entry.

The client wanted to lead with a stronger discount than comparable products in the market.

My challenge

Could low pricing reduce trust?

I believed a very low price for a new health product could create doubt around quality and perceived value for some customers.

The change

Build stronger value signals.

After the initial approach underperformed, I proposed branded gift items, stronger creator/UGC activity and credibility-focused awareness work.

Outcome

Short-term sales uplift.

The repositioned approach was associated with a 25% sales uplift over the expected baseline used for this case.

Founder · Eltwkeel

Building and scaling an e-commerce business from the ground up.

Eltwkeel started as a small mattress e-commerce operation. I gradually became responsible for the store, growth strategy, SEO, sales operations, supplier relationships, customer experience, marketplace work and the move into physical retail.

2physical branches
~EGP 3Mlatest full-year sales across online + offline
4mattress brands in the partner portfolio
5.05KGoogle Search Console clicks in the Aug 2025 28-day snapshot
01 · Build

The store became the foundation.

Built and managed the WordPress/WooCommerce setup, products, product architecture, pricing, payment gateways, shipping logic and conversion improvements.

02 · Trust

Sell advice, not only mattresses.

Built a proposition around original products, consultation, inspection, sample models, flexible storage/delivery and a more reassuring buying experience for a high-consideration product.

03 · Partnerships

Reduce supplier concentration risk.

Started with Taki, then expanded the partner portfolio to Taki, Habitat, Wonderland and Aldora — evaluating product quality, warranty, delivery, pricing and fit with the brand vision.

04 · Organic growth

Make SEO a cost-efficient growth engine.

Managed an SEO team and used recurring reports to build search visibility and organic demand when sustained paid acquisition was difficult to afford.

05 · Sales operations

Build the system around the buying journey.

Created a lightweight CRM workflow to organize leads, follow-ups, ownership, order status, payments, shipping and reporting — using automation where it could remove repetitive work.

06 · Expansion

From online to multi-channel.

Extended the business into marketplace/Vendor work, B2B opportunities and physical retail, eventually operating with two branches.
