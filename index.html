The funnel looked healthy — sales did not.

The product attracted attention, but sales stayed weak. Clarity showed users spending a long time on the product page without taking action.

Customer discovery

Use customer questions as evidence.

04 / MAKASEB

MOKNH ↗

Offer Strategy · Consumer Psychology · Wellness

A new wellness brand with one core product. The client wanted a stronger discount than similar products; I challenged whether low pricing would actually build trust for a new health product.

PricingPerceived ValueOffer StrategyUGCCreators
+25%sales uplift agreed for the case outcome
1core product
UGCcreator-led credibility

Initial assumption

Lower price = easier entry.

The launch was initially positioned around a stronger discount.

My challenge

Could low pricing create doubt?

I believed a very low price for a new health product could weaken perceived value and trust for some buyers.

The change

Build stronger value signals.

After the initial approach underperformed, I proposed branded gifts, creator/UGC activity and credibility-building campaigns.

Outcome

+25% sales uplift.

The repositioned approach was associated with the short-term improvement used for this case.
